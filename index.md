# OrgHop: Quick search for Salesforce

Jump to any Salesforce Setup page, app, object or record in one keystroke. Press Alt+S, type a few letters and press Enter.

OrgHop uses your existing Salesforce login and talks only to your own org. It has no servers, accounts, analytics or ads.
