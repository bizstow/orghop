Pinned pages show at the top of OrgHop under **Pinned**, before you type anything.

## Pin a page

1. Open OrgHop and find the Setup page, for example by typing "flows".
2. Select it with the arrow keys and press <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd>, or click the star at the end of its row.

The star turns gold. Do the same again to unpin it.

## Pins follow you

Pins are saved in your Chrome profile. If Chrome sync is on, they appear in your other browsers too. OrgHop stores only which pages you pinned, such as "Users" or "Flows", and nothing about your org.
