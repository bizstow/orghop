Keyboard shortcuts

Every key OrgHop understands, and how to change the ones that open it.

Opening OrgHop

ShortcutWhat it does
Alt SOpen the OrgHop popup from the toolbar
Cmd Shift K on macOS, Ctrl Shift K elsewhereOpen the search overlay on top of the Salesforce page

In the results

ShortcutWhat it does
↑ ↓, Page Up Page DownMove through the results
EnterOpen the selected result in your org's tab
Cmd or Ctrl + EnterOpen it in a new tab (a middle click does the same)
Shift EnterPin or unpin the selected Setup page
EscClear the search, then press again to close OrgHop

Changing the shortcuts

Click the shortcut button in OrgHop's footer, or go to chrome://extensions/shortcuts, and set the keys you want for "Open OrgHop" and the search overlay.
